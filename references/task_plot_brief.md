# Task Plot Brief

Title: Approximate Number System
Construct: non-symbolic numerosity discrimination

## Participant-Visible Flow

- Global flow: Chinese instructions teach `F = yellow more` and `J = blue more`; each of two blocks begins with 10 practice trials, then 40 test trials; only practice gives correctness feedback.
- Common test-trial phases: fixation (500 ms) -> intermixed blue/yellow dots (200 ms) -> blank response continuation (up to 4 s total from dot onset, only if unanswered) -> blank ITI (500 ms).
- Dot sets share one centered aperture; colors are intermixed, not separated left/right.

## Representative Rows

- `1:2 · equal dot size`: 5 yellow dots and 10 blue dots; blue is more numerous; all dots same diameter.
- `3:4 · equal total area`: 6 blue dots and 8 yellow dots; yellow is more numerous; yellow dots are smaller so the two colors have equal cumulative area.
- `5:6 · equal total area`: 6 blue dots and 5 yellow dots; blue is more numerous; blue dots are smaller so the two colors have equal cumulative area.
- `7:8 · equal dot size`: 7 blue dots and 8 yellow dots; yellow is more numerous; all dots same diameter.

## Exact Labels

- Phase labels: `FIXATION`, `DOTS`, `RESPOND`, `ITI`.
- Timing labels: `500 ms`, `200 ms`, `4 s total`, `500 ms`.
- Key reminder may appear only as a small note outside participant screens: `F yellow · J blue`.
- Practice branch note: `Practice only: 500 ms feedback`.
